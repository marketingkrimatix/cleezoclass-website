### A connected admission process, from enquiry to enrolment

Admissions involve more than an online form. School teams need a reliable way to record enquiries, collect registration details, manage the next step, and ensure accepted applicants become part of the school’s active student record.

Cleezo Class connects those stages in one admission management system. Teams can manage walk-ins, QR registrations, Excel imports, tracking IDs, entrance and counselling workflows, and the movement of accepted enquiries into enrolled student records.

---

### Capture and organise admission enquiries

Give the admissions team a consistent place to begin every prospective-student journey.

- Record walk-in enquiries through the admission workflow.
- Use QR registration when the school needs a quick registration path for prospective parents and students.
- Import enquiry data from Excel when the school already has approved prospect data to add.
- Use tracking IDs to organise and follow individual enquiries.

This helps teams avoid losing the context of an enquiry as it moves between the front desk, counselling and admission teams.

**CTA:** Discuss Your Admission Workflow

### Manage entrance and counselling stages

Different schools use different evaluation and counselling processes. Cleezo Class keeps the applicant’s tracking ID and admission-stage progress in the same admission record as the enquiry moves through entrance and counselling stages.

Admissions users can see where an enquiry is in the school’s process and continue working from the relevant applicant information until the school makes its admission decision.

---

### Turn accepted enquiries into enrolled student records

When an applicant is accepted, the transition into the school should not require a disconnected data-entry process. Cleezo Class supports the conversion of an accepted enquiry into an enrolled student record.

That record becomes part of the broader [student information system](/student-information-system/), where the school can manage student information, attendance, academics, fees and parent access through connected workflows.

---

### Follow up through the school’s official WhatsApp number

Prospective-parent follow-up needs consistency and appropriate oversight. When a school pairs its official WhatsApp number with Cleezo Class, admissions users can use the school-owned number for enquiry tracking and follow-up instead of relying on personal messaging accounts.

Here, tracking means keeping the follow-up activity connected to the relevant admission enquiry. Admissions staff manage these follow-ups through the school’s official WhatsApp number.

### FAQs

#### What is admission management software for schools?

Admission management software helps a school organise the process from a prospective family’s first enquiry through registration, counselling or entrance steps, admission decisions and enrolment. It gives admission teams a structured workflow instead of relying on separate spreadsheets and disconnected communication.

#### Can Cleezo Class manage walk-in admissions and QR registrations?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports walk-in enquiries and QR registration as part of its admission workflow. The right process can be configured around the school’s admission operations.

#### Can existing enquiry data be added to the platform?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports Excel imports for approved enquiry data. During implementation, the school and implementation team should agree on the data fields, import responsibility and validation steps.

#### What can admissions teams do during entrance and counselling stages?

Admissions teams can keep the applicant’s tracking ID and admission-stage progress with the same enquiry record as it moves through entrance and counselling stages. This gives teams a clearer view of where the applicant is in the school’s defined admission process.

#### Can an accepted applicant become an enrolled student record?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports the conversion of an accepted enquiry into an enrolled student record. That record can then be managed through the connected [student information system](/student-information-system/).

#### Can admission teams use WhatsApp for follow-up?

Yes. A school can pair its authorised WhatsApp number with Cleezo Class so admissions staff can follow up with prospective parents through a school-owned channel and keep follow-up activity connected to the relevant enquiry.

#### Is Cleezo Class suitable for multi-campus admission operations?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports multi-branch and multi-campus institutions with role-appropriate workspaces and centralisation workflows. A demo can be configured around the institution’s admissions structure.
