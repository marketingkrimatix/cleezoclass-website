## What is school timetable software?

**School timetable software** helps a school organise classes, subjects, teachers, sections and periods into an academic schedule. Rather than building every timetable manually in spreadsheets, academic teams can work from the rules and constraints that shape a practical school day.

For schools evaluating an online timetable generator, a useful timetable is more than a grid of periods. It needs to reflect the availability of teachers, the number of sections, scheduled breaks, subject requirements and the changes that arise during the term.

---

## When manual timetable planning becomes difficult

Manual timetables are often difficult to update because one change can affect several teachers, sections and periods. Academic teams need to balance subject allocation with real teaching availability while keeping the timetable clear enough for day-to-day use.

Cleezo helps schools bring that planning into their wider school operations instead of treating the timetable as an isolated spreadsheet.

| Planning challenge | What the timetable must account for |
| --- | --- |
| Teacher allocation | Which teacher is available for each subject and period |
| Multiple sections | How classes are scheduled across the required sections |
| Breaks and school-day structure | Where breaks fit within the period plan |
| Subject weightage | How often a subject needs to appear in the timetable |
| Changes during the term | How teams review scheduling clashes and coordinate substitute-teacher arrangements |

## A practical timetable-planning checklist

Before setting up a timetable, confirm the information the academic team needs:

1. Which sections and periods need to be scheduled?
2. Which teachers are allocated to each subject, and when are they available?
3. What break structure should the timetable follow?
4. What subject weightage should each section receive during the week?
5. How should the team manage a clash or teacher absence?
6. Which users need access to create, review or use the timetable?

Discuss these requirements with academic coordinators, teachers and administrators before a demo so Cleezo can be configured around your school’s operating model.
