## H2: A student fee management system connected to school operations

Fee work affects admissions, student records, parent communication and school administration. A student fee management system should therefore make it easier for authorised teams to review the information they need around a student’s fee record, rather than relying on disconnected registers and follow-up lists.

Cleezo Class brings fee-related records into the wider school workflow, with role-based workspaces for the people responsible for finance and administration.

## H2: Manage the fee details your school needs to track

### H3: Class-wise fee heads

Organise fee structures through class-wise fee heads, so the school can maintain the fee components that apply to each class in one system.

### H3: Merit, sibling and referral concessions

Manage merit, sibling and referral concessions against the relevant student fee record. This gives authorised staff a clearer place to review the concession details used in day-to-day fee administration.

### H3: Outstanding dues and recorded payments

Review recorded payments and outstanding dues so accounts teams can see each student’s fee position without switching between disconnected records.

### H3: Fee receipts

Record payments against the relevant student and generate receipts. Keep payment details and receipt records connected to the student’s fee account.

### H3: Record payment methods and review collections

Record payments made by cash, UPI, cheque and bank transfer. Finance teams can review the recorded collection entries and outstanding dues as part of their regular fee process.

Give the relevant school teams access through their role-based workspace, so fee administration can sit within the wider operating system for the school.

## H2: For Indian schools: distinguish payment recording from a payment gateway

Schools in India may receive and record payments through cash, UPI, cheque and bank transfer. Cleezo Class supports recording those payment methods and reviewing the recorded collection entries and outstanding dues as part of fee administration.

Payment recording is different from an integrated online payment gateway or automatic bank reconciliation. If your school requires online payment collection, bank-statement matching or settlement reconciliation, ask the Cleezo Class team to demonstrate the required functionality during your demo.

## H2: What the fee workflow can look like

1. Configure class-wise fee heads and student fee details.
2. Apply applicable merit, sibling or referral concessions.
3. Record the payment received.
4. Issue the relevant receipt and retain the payment record against the student.
5. Review recorded collections and outstanding dues as part of the finance process.
6. Make relevant information available to authorised finance and school-administration teams in their respective workspaces.

The exact workflow should reflect your school’s processes. Book a demo to discuss the records, roles and payment methods your team needs to manage.

## H2: Frequently asked questions

### H3: What is school fee management software?

School fee management software helps a school organise class-wise fee heads, concessions, payment records, outstanding dues and receipts in a more structured workflow. The exact requirements vary by school.

### H3: What is a student fee management system?

A student fee management system is software used to maintain fee information in the context of each student record. For a school, it should support the finance and administration workflows that sit around those records.

### H3: Can schools configure different fee heads for different classes?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports class-wise fee heads, helping schools organise the fee components that apply to each class.

### H3: Can Cleezo Class manage concessions and outstanding dues?

Yes. Schools can manage merit, sibling and referral concessions, and accounts teams can review outstanding dues.

### H3: Can parents view their child’s fee status?

Yes. Cleezo Class provides parent and student fee-status access so families can view the relevant fee information for the student.

### H3: Can Cleezo Class record UPI, cheque, cash and bank-transfer payments?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports recording cash, UPI, cheque and bank-transfer payments as part of fee administration.
