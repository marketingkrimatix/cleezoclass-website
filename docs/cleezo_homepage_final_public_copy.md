## The school management modules your teams use every day

### Admissions CRM and enrolment

Capture walk-in enquiries, QR registrations and Excel lead imports in one pipeline. Give every applicant a tracking number, record entrance-test and counselling outcomes, schedule WhatsApp follow-ups, and convert accepted applicants into student records without re-entering their details.

### Fee collection and accounting

Create class-wise fee structures for tuition, admission, transport, sports, term or lab fees. Record payments by cash, UPI, cheque or bank transfer, issue branded QR-verifiable receipts, and manage approved concessions such as merit, sibling or referral discounts.

See outstanding fees clearly with fee-status, arrears, daybook, defaulter and student-passbook reports. Accounts teams can also track expenses and vendor or party ledgers in the same system.

### Academic and examination management

Build timetables, manage substitute allocations, prepare examinations, generate seating arrangements and hall tickets, and publish custom report cards. Cleezo includes six configurable report-card templates with mark breakdowns, attendance percentages, charts and performance trends.

### Attendance, HR and payroll

Synchronise student and staff attendance from supported networked biometric or RFID devices. Track late or irregular attendance and leave workflows, then manage salary components, deductions, PF, allowances, bonuses and salary slips in the same platform.

### Parent communication and school updates

Give parents and students access to fee status, digital report cards and event updates. Schools can pair their official WhatsApp number to send fee receipts, attendance alerts and admission or promotional updates.

### Multi-branch centralisation

For school groups and educational trusts, Cleezo provides one central workspace for campus setup, master data, bulk Excel import, student and staff directories, and institutional database backups.

## Automation for the school tasks that create the most manual work

### Conflict-aware timetable generation

Cleezo’s timetable engine uses teacher availability, class sections, periods, break intervals and subject weightage to generate weekly timetables. It identifies teacher overlaps and helps administrators find available substitutes when staff are absent.

### Curriculum-aware question-paper generation

Build question papers from chapter and syllabus topics, choose formats such as MCQs, short answers and long answers, set marks allocation, and export branded PDFs for school examinations.

### AI-assisted student photo preparation

When staff upload or capture a student photo, Cleezo can remove a non-white background on the device, add a clean white background and format the image for school ID-card workflows.

### Student performance trends

Report-card views can show multi-term subject trends, charts and performance indicators so teachers and parents can review progress beyond a single mark sheet.

## School management software FAQs

### What is Cleezo Class?

Cleezo Class is a cloud-based school management system for K–12 schools, private academies and multi-branch educational institutions. It brings admissions CRM, fee and expense management, academics, attendance, HR/payroll and parent communication into connected, role-based workspaces.

### Which types of schools can use Cleezo?

Cleezo supports private K–12 schools, academies, educational trusts and multi-campus school groups, including CBSE, ICSE, SSC and State Board institutions.

### Can Cleezo manage multiple school branches?

Yes. Cleezo includes a centralisation workspace for multi-campus institutions. Central teams can set up campuses, manage master data, import records in bulk and maintain institution-wide student and staff directories.

### Can we import existing student, staff and admission data?

Yes. Cleezo supports Excel import and export for student, staff and lead data. Your implementation plan should confirm the fields, data cleaning and validation required for your existing records.

### How does Cleezo handle school fees?

Schools can configure class-wise fee heads, record cash, UPI, cheque and bank-transfer payments, issue branded QR-verifiable receipts, set approved concessions and review dues, daybook, defaulter, expense and ledger reports.

### Does Cleezo support biometric attendance and payroll?

Yes. Cleezo supports synchronisation with networked biometric and RFID attendance devices for students and staff. HR teams can track attendance and leave, set salary components and deductions, and generate salary slips.

### What does the timetable tool do?

The timetable engine considers teacher availability, sections, periods, breaks and subject weightage when creating weekly schedules. It flags teacher overlaps and can support substitute allocation when staff are absent.

### Can schools communicate through WhatsApp?

Schools can pair their official WhatsApp number with Cleezo to send fee receipts, attendance alerts and admission or promotional messages. The exact communication workflow and permissions can be configured for the school.

### How is Cleezo priced?

Cleezo plans are selected according to the number of students and the modules a school needs. Request a demo to review the appropriate configuration and commercial proposal.
