## What is a student attendance management system?

A **student attendance management system** brings attendance information into an organised school workflow. Instead of checking separate records across departments, authorised staff can review the attendance information associated with students and connect it with relevant school administration tasks.

For schools using biometric or RFID devices, the important step is connecting supported device records with the wider student information system—not simply capturing a fingerprint or card scan.

---

## Why attendance records become difficult to manage

An attendance record may come from a school device while the student profile, follow-up and report card are managed elsewhere. School teams then have to piece information together across different workflows.

Cleezo Class connects student attendance with the broader school ERP environment so teams can review records and use relevant information in student administration and parent communication.

## Attendance review, student information and reporting

### Review class-wise attendance and exceptions

The **class-wise student attendance register** shows student name, class, section, present days, informed and uninformed days, total days and attendance percentage. School teams can also review student absentees, leave requests and irregular-attendance entries, including informed versus uninformed absences.

Available review controls include **class, section and month selection** in attendance reporting, a **date picker** in the director's leave workflow, an **informed/uninformed** filter in irregular-attendance review, and text search in the class-wise report. Controls vary by screen and user role.

### Use monthly reports and attendance exports

The monthly student attendance report provides a **six-month present-versus-total-days view** and a **three-month rolling average attendance percentage**.

Attendance reporting includes **Excel (.xlsx), PDF and PNG exports** in the administrative reports area. The separate irregular-attendance view supports a tab-separated **.xls** download and printing. These formats belong to their respective screens; they are not promised for every attendance report.

### Keep records connected with student information

Attendance is part of the wider [**Student Information System**](/student-information-system/), where schools also manage enrolment details and academic information. Relevant access depends on school user roles.

### Include attendance percentages on report cards

Cleezo Class supports report-card templates that can show **attendance percentages** alongside academic results. Report-card attendance percentages are a distinct capability from the class-wise and monthly attendance reports described above.

### Support parent attendance updates

Schools can use their paired official WhatsApp workflow for attendance-related parent alerts. The school needs to complete the communication pairing and permissions as part of setup.

## Student attendance management FAQs

### Can Cleezo Class integrate with biometric and RFID attendance devices?

Yes. Cleezo Class supports integration with compatible networked biometric and RFID attendance devices. Confirm the device model and connection requirements before setup.

### Can schools review attendance exceptions?

Yes. The documented student-information workflow provides for reviewing student attendance records and attendance exceptions. Specific correction permissions and steps should be confirmed in a product demonstration.

### Can parents receive attendance alerts?

Cleezo Class supports attendance-related alerts through the school's paired official WhatsApp workflow. Pairing and permissions are set up for the school.

### How does attendance connect with student records and report cards?

Attendance records are part of the connected student information workflow. Schools can review attendance alongside student profiles, and supported report-card templates can display attendance percentages alongside academic results.

### Can teachers manually mark student attendance without biometric or RFID hardware?

No. The audited Cleezo Class teacher web workspace does not include a manual daily student-attendance register or present/absent marking screen. The documented attendance-capture workflow uses connected biometric or RFID devices.

### Which attendance reports and downloads are available?

Cleezo Class includes a class-wise attendance register, student absentees and leave records, irregular-attendance review and a monthly attendance report. The administrative reporting area supports Excel, PDF and PNG downloads; irregular-attendance review also offers a tab-separated .xls download and printing. Available filters and exports depend on the particular screen and user role.
